# TeamPad One-Pager

## One-Liner
TeamPad is the calm, fast workspace where team chat and knowledge live together — built for small teams that need speed without chaos.

## Problem
Teams lose context across chat threads and scattered docs. Decisions get buried, ownership is unclear, and updates are hard to trace.

## Solution
TeamPad combines realtime workspace chat with structured notes, keeping teams aligned with a single, searchable source of truth.

## What Makes It Different
- Chat + notes in the same workspace, not split across tools.
- Fast, mobile-first UX that avoids blank states.
- Realtime updates across web + mobile.
- Simple roles + invites for real teams.

## MVP Success Metrics (30 Days)
- Activation: 70% of invited users create or edit a note.
- Retention: 40% weekly active teams.
- Engagement: 3+ notes and 15+ chat messages per workspace.

## Why Not Slack / Notion
- Slack = fast chat, weak long-term memory.
- Notion = structured docs, slow for quick coordination.
- TeamPad merges both in one focused workspace.

## Who It’s For
- Small teams (2–20) collaborating daily.
- Product, ops, and marketing teams.
- Founders who need a fast, structured knowledge hub.

## Core Features (MVP)
- Workspaces + collections + notes (create/edit/pin/search).
- Workspace chat with reactions + realtime updates.
- Invites, roles, and secure access.
- Mobile-first UX with fast loading and no blank states.

## Monetization (Draft)
- Free: 1 workspace, 5 collections, 8 notes per collection, 3 versions, 1k chat retention.
- Premium: 3 workspaces, 20 collections, 200 notes per collection, 10 versions, 3k retention.
- Premium+: unlimited, public notes, 20 versions, 10k retention (AI planned).
